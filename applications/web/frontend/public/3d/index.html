Intelligent parking & traffic control

Dhaka has
a pulse.

CityPulse reads it from the cameras the city already has — free spaces for drivers, steady income for lot owners, and clear lanes for traffic authorities.

02 The problem

One car on the curb.
One lane gone.

When drivers can't find parking, they stop wherever they can. One car on the road edge squeezes a lane, and the queue behind it can last for hours — while the lot beside it still has free spaces nobody can see.

  • Roadside parking
  • Lane blocked
  • Left-hand traffic

03 Sense

Use the cameras
already on the wall.

Parking owners register their lot and connect the CCTV they already run. No sensors in the ground and no new hardware — CityPulse takes the camera's RTSP stream.

  • Existing CCTV
  • RTSP stream
  • Owner portal

04 Detect

Every space,
empty or occupied.

Each space is cut from the camera frame and read by a MobileNetV2 model trained on 808,991 parking-space images. On a car park it never saw in training, it is right 99.18% of the time.

05 Guide

Straight to
a free space.

Drivers open the app, see free spaces near them, book one, and drive to the entrance. A booked space is never shown as free to anyone else.

06 Enforce Next phase

See the jam.
Find its cause.

Next, the same cameras go to work on the road. At busy junctions they flag the vehicle causing a jam and read its plate in Bangla and English, so traffic authorities can act on a live congestion map instead of posting police at every corner.

  • Congestion heat map
  • Bangla + English plates
  • Enforcement log

07 Impact

Every free space.
The whole city.

Each lot that joins lights up on the same live map. Spare spaces that sat empty beside blocked roads become places drivers can find, and income their owners can count.

Why it matters

Sensed, not typed.

Traffic in Dhaka now averages about 7 km/h, and roadside parking is one of the biggest reasons. Every parking app in Bangladesh still waits for someone to type in a count. CityPulse reads it from the camera instead.

  1. TodayWith CityPulse

  2. Finding a space

    TodayDrivers circle the block, give up, and stop on the road — taking a lane with them.

    With CityPulseFree spaces show on the map before you set out, and the app takes you to the entrance.

  3. Keeping the count right

    TodaySomeone updates the count by hand. It falls behind exactly when the lot is busiest.

    With CityPulseThe lot's own CCTV updates every space by itself. Nobody has to sit at a keyboard.

  4. Spare private spaces

    TodayGarages and private lots sit half-empty beside jammed roads, because nobody can find them.

    With CityPulseOwners list their spaces once. Drivers can find them, and every booking is income.

  5. Bookings

    TodayA space that looks free may already be promised to someone else.

    With CityPulseA booked space is never shown as free. The booking always wins over what the camera sees.

  6. Policing the road

    TodayOfficers stand at every busy junction through heat, monsoon rain and smoke.

    With CityPulseAuthorities watch parking across zones from one command centre, and act where it matters.

How it's built

From camera to phone.

Apps only ever talk to the backend. The backend sends each camera image to the AI service, checks the answer against live bookings, and saves the result — so every screen shows the same number.

  1. CCTV imagefrom the lot's own camera
  2. AI serviceempty or occupied, with a confidence
  3. Backendchecks bookings, saves the answer
  4. Driver's mapfree spaces near you
